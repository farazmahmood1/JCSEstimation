Accurate cost estimation prevents cost overruns:

Accurate cost estimation is critical to preventing cost overruns in construction projects. When contractors underestimate the costs involved in a project, they risk running into unexpected expenses later on, which can lead to delays, extra expenses, and ultimately a lower profit margin for the contractor.

If the contractor did not account for these contingencies in their original cost estimate, the project can quickly become more expensive than anticipated, leading to cost overruns.

Difference between Constructions Bidding and Construction Estimation:

Construction bid and construction estimation are two distinct but related aspects of the construction process. Here are the key differences between them.

  • The primary purpose of a constructions bid is to formally offer to perform a construction project for a specified cost and timeline. In contrast, the purpose of construction estimation is to determine the cost and timeline of a construction project before it is put out to bid.
  • Construction bids are typically based on detailed project specifications, drawings, and plans provided by the client or the architect. Construction estimations, on the other hand, are based on preliminary information and conceptual designs, which may be subject to change.
  •  Construction bids typically provide a much higher level of detail than construction estimations, including a detailed description of the scope of work, labor, materials, and equipment required to complete the project.
  • Constructions bids are legally binding documents, whereas construction estimations are not. A construction bid becomes a legally binding contract when it is accepted by the client.
  • Constructions bids are used to select the contractor for a specific project, and competition between contractors is a key aspect of the bid process. Construction estimations, on the other hand, are typically prepared by a single contractor or construction consultant and are not intended for use in a competitive bidding process
