Important 6 steps in estimate construction bid:

There are different steps involved in the calculation of a construction bid. Following factors listed below are the ones that are involved in the bid:

Project review:

A detailed review of the entire project plan and its analysis. This involves a clear understanding of the system specifications, resources, material, cost allocation, deadline, and management.

Quantify the material required:

Based on the project plan analysis, further on how to estimate construction bid you need to calculate the quantity required for required materials such as cement, steel, iron, concrete, electrical supplies, and machinery. It is very crucial to understand the quantity and calculate the amount to include in the budget.

Calculate the pricing and budget:

After having a clear view of the specifications required, contact suppliers and business owners who supply relevant material to have a clear and updated idea about the price of material and equipment. You can collect any discounts that suppliers offer for your purchase. These discounts also play an important role to estimate construction bids.

Don’t forget to add direct and indirect costs:

Always subdivide your expenses into direct and indirect costs. The direct cost refers to those expenses that play a direct hand in your bid calculation such as office, guards, maintenance, and repair. Both of these factors go hand in hand in the guide of how to estimate a construction bid.

Estimate the factors in profit margin:

The calculation of profit margin also plays a crucial role in determining how to estimate a construction bid. The profit that will be gained on the project completion is also added to the estimation bid. This immensely gives the contractor an overview of the results of his investment in the project.

Bid presentation: 

Submit the complete bid, including all the costs, work breakdown structure, timeline, and entire budget. Develop the bid using these factors to indicate an estimate of the construction bid
